# How to reply to YY

YY reads on a phone and does not want walls of text.

- Reply in 3 short lines or fewer.
- Show data as a picture: update the i004 Desk (https://claude.ai/artifact/7FwEa2iuXav7V98gsQr17a) or send a chart image. No tables unless asked.
- Lead with the answer. No background, no "why", no option lists unless YY asks.
- Ask at most one question, as a yes/no or pick-one.
